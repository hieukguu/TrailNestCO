## Image Guidelines

1. **Never use AI-generated images** as if they were real product photos.
2. **Acceptable sources:**
   - Official brand press kits (request via media/affiliate contact)
   - Amazon product images (check licensing terms — generally cannot be hotlinked)
   - Creative Commons licensed photos with proper attribution
   - Original photography of products you physically own
3. **Image naming convention:** `[slug]-hero.jpg`, `[slug]-pick-1.jpg`, etc.
4. **Store in:** `public/images/[article-slug]/`
5. **alt text:** Descriptive, include product name. Avoid keyword stuffing.

---

## Unsplash Images — Current Usage

Unsplash images are used under the Unsplash License (free for commercial use, no attribution required). They are acceptable as placeholder images but do not represent the actual reviewed products. Replacing them with actual product images will improve credibility and user trust.
