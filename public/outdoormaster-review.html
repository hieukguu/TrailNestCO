OutdoorMaster FAQ

Q

Is the SHARK 3 really faster than competitor electric pumps?

In our testing and across verified third-party reviews (GearJunkie, CNN Underscored), the SHARK 3 consistently inflated a 10'6" board in 3–4 minutes. Competing pumps in the same price range averaged 6–9 minutes. The HyFlow™ dual-stage technology is the key differentiator — it's not marketing, it's measurable.

Q

What's the difference between MIPS and non-MIPS helmets?

MIPS (Multi-directional Impact Protection System) adds a low-friction layer inside the helmet that allows the shell to rotate independently of your head during an angled impact. This reduces rotational brain injury risk by up to 10–15%. OutdoorMaster's GEM MIPS at $65.99 gives you this same certified protection that normally costs $150+ from brands like Giro or Bell.

Q

Which OutdoorMaster sunglasses are best for road cycling?

For road cycling, the HAWK LT ($79) is our top recommendation — the photochromic lens adjusts automatically as you move from tree shade to open road, which is exactly what road cyclists deal with. If budget allows, the PHOENIX X with ZEISS lenses ($139.99) offers noticeably sharper optics that reduce eye fatigue on long rides.

Q

What pump should I pair with the OutdoorMaster SUP board?

The SHARK 3 ($179.99) is the natural pairing — it's purpose-built for OutdoorMaster boards and inflates faster than any included manual pump. If you're budget-conscious, the SHARK II ($99.99) is fully compatible and still dramatically faster than hand-pumping. All SHARK models work with the 10'6" board.

Q

Will the Horse Journey Golf Travel Case fit airline overhead requirements?

The Horse Journey is designed for checked baggage, not overhead — it protects your clubs in the hold where the real risk is. It meets standard airline checked luggage dimensions and includes a TSA-approved lock. Airlines treat it as an oversized sports equipment bag, which typically incurs a $25–$50 fee depending on your carrier and ticket class.

Q

Is OutdoorMaster gear available outside the US?

OutdoorMaster ships to select international destinations, but availability varies by product. The SUP board is US-only per current listings. Most cycling helmets, sunglasses, and golf cases ship internationally. Check your cart for your country — they also sell through Amazon in multiple marketplaces which often has broader international availability.
