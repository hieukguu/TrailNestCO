Field notes

Plan the kit, not just the outfit.

What works best for hot-weather hiking?

Start with NoFry for sun coverage, then choose Vent Pants for full leg coverage or Virtue Shorts when your route includes water.

Which pant is best for tougher terrain?

Shenanigan Pants are the more durable, trail-focused choice; Vent Pants prioritise low weight and airflow in heat.

Can this gear work for camping and travel?

Yes. The edit is built around versatile pieces, with Tomfoolery Chinos and MilesBound Hoodie especially useful beyond the trail.
