FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Common questions about Sunjoy gazebos and outdoor structures.

How long does assembly take for the Hampton 12×16 Gazebo?
Most customers complete assembly in 2.5–3 hours with two people. Sunjoy pre-drills all holes and includes all hardware, so you're not improvising. If you prefer professional setup, Sunjoy partners with Assembly Help for a white-glove installation service.
Can the Hampton Gazebo handle snow?
Yes — the dual-tier galvanized steel roof is engineered for all-season use, including light-to-moderate snow loads. The ventilated ridge helps prevent ice buildup. For heavy snow regions (more than 30 lbs/sq ft), we recommend clearing the roof after major storms as a precaution.
Is the cedar frame treated or sealed at the factory?
The cedar posts and beams are natural — the steel roof panels are factory powder-coated for rust resistance. We recommend applying an exterior wood sealant to the cedar frame annually or every two years, especially in wet or humid climates, to maintain appearance and longevity.
Can I hang a ceiling fan inside the gazebo?
Sunjoy does not officially recommend hanging a ceiling fan from the Hampton 12×16 — the center point height is 9.2 ft, and the structure isn't engineered for the continuous vibration of a fan motor. The built-in ceiling hooks are rated for chandeliers, string lights, hanging baskets, and similar static loads.
What size expansion bolts are needed for concrete anchoring?
M6 expansion bolts. Anchoring to a concrete patio is highly recommended for permanent installations — it dramatically improves wind stability and safety in exposed backyard locations.
What's Sunjoy's return policy?
Sunjoy offers 30-day returns on all orders. Large LTL items are delivered curbside — inspect boxes on arrival before the delivery driver leaves, and document any damage with photos before starting assembly.
